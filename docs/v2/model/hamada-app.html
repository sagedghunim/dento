Online consultations

Patients who cannot come in can still talk to the doctor. Requests land here, next to the rest of the day's work.

Requests

How it works

  1. The patient asks for an online consultation and picks a time.
  2. The request appears here and in Today's activity, so nobody misses it.
  3. Accept, then start the call at the agreed time. Notes and a prescription can follow the same visit.

Follow-ups after a procedure — like checking an extraction a day later — are the most common use in dental clinics.
